Third-party motion libraries vendored for CHRAIM Campus
======================================================

All files are served same-origin (no CDN at runtime) so the enforced CSP
`script-src 'self'` keeps working. The builds are unmodified copies from the
published npm packages.

GSAP 3.15.1 + ScrollTrigger 3.15.1
  https://gsap.com
  License: Standard "no charge" license — https://gsap.com/standard-license
  Files: gsap-3.15.1.min.js, ScrollTrigger-3.15.1.min.js

Lenis 1.3.27
  https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis
  License: MIT — see lenis-LICENSE.txt
  File: lenis-1.3.27.min.js

Consumers: /assets/motion.js, /assets/motion.css (loaded by index.html only).
Removing those two files and the four script/link tags restores the previous
behaviour with no other change.
